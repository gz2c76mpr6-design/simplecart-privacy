Privacy Policy Last Updated: September 13, 2026 This Privacy Policy explains how ExamTrack ("the App") handles information when you use the App. ExamTrack is operated by ExamTrack Developer. Contact: simplecart83@gmail.com 1. Information You Enter ExamTrack allows you to enter and store study and examination-related information, including: - Exam name - Exam date - Target study hours - Weekly study goals - Theme color - Study subjects - Study duration - Study notes - Dates and times associated with study sessions This information is used solely to provide the App's features, including study tracking, progress calculations, statistics, calendar views, reminders, and widgets. 2. How Your Data Is Stored Exam and study-session information is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData/SQLite technology. The App may also use Apple's App Group shared container to allow the main application and its widget extension to access the necessary data. The App does not use iCloud or CloudKit to synchronize your study data. The App does not operate its own server or database for storing your study records. Certain application settings, such as notification preferences, theme preferences, language selection, premium status, and other local application state, are stored locally on your device. When you export study data as a CSV file, the file is temporarily created on your device and is shared only when you explicitly choose to use the iOS share function. 3. Advertising The free version of ExamTrack uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as: - IP address - Device or advertising identifiers - Advertising and interaction information - Diagnostic information - Performance information - Information related to advertisements displayed or interacted with This information may be processed by Google and its advertising partners for purposes such as serving advertisements, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, and improving advertising services. ExamTrack does not send your exam names, study notes, or individual study records to Google for advertising purposes. For more information about Google's handling of data, please refer to Google's privacy policies. 4. App Tracking Transparency ExamTrack does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not directly access the IDFA. Advertising behavior and data handling may nevertheless depend on the configuration and behavior of the Google Mobile Ads SDK and applicable platform settings. 5. In-App Purchases ExamTrack uses Apple's StoreKit to provide premium features. Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple. ExamTrack does not receive or store your payment card information. Apple may process transaction-related information necessary to complete and manage purchases. 5. No User Accounts ExamTrack does not require you to create an account. The App does not directly collect your name, email address, telephone number, or password. 6. Analytics and Crash Reporting ExamTrack does not intentionally integrate third-party analytics or crash-reporting services such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Sentry, or Mixpanel. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect diagnostic and performance information as part of its operation. 7. Notifications and Other Features ExamTrack may use Apple's local notification system to provide reminders that you configure in the App. The App does not use a remote server to send push notifications and does not require a device push token for its reminder functionality. CSV export and sharing are performed only when you explicitly initiate those actions. Siri and App Intents functionality, where available, operates locally on the device. 8. Data Retention and Deletion ExamTrack stores your exam and study information locally on your device. The App currently does not provide an in-app function to delete individual exam or study-session records. If you uninstall the App, locally stored App data is generally removed by iOS, subject to Apple's operating-system behavior and any data that may have been shared or exported by you. Third-Party Services ExamTrack uses the following third-party services: - Google AdMob for advertising - Apple StoreKit for in-app purchases and subscriptions These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms. 10. Children's Privacy ExamTrack is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through user accounts or registration because the App does not require an account. 11. Security We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Because most study information is stored locally on your device, the security of your device, operating system, and device passcode also plays an important role in protecting your information. 12.
